From one sentence to a package you can post

Each step says plainly whether it works today or is coming next.

  1. Pick a niche

    Choose one of ten ready-made niches or describe your own. The concierge tells you whether the studio can produce it in full, or only research and draft it.

    Works today

  2. The team gets to work

    A researcher stores sourced facts, a strategist plans formats, a scriptwriter drafts and a reviewer checks the draft. They pass work to each other over the open A2A protocol.

    Agents work today, research storage comes next

  3. You approve, then it moves on

    Each video waits for you. You get a ready-to-post package for every platform you chose, with a checklist and the platform's own rules.

    Packages work today, YouTube connection comes next
