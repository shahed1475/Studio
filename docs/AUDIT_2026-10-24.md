## 5. Research (what the outside world says)
- **Official, read 24 Oct (VERIFIED):** YouTube's monetization policy page (renamed "repetitious" to "inauthentic content" on 15 Jul 2025). Not allowed: "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production without adding the creator's original, authentic insights or perspective". Allowed: the same intro and outro when the bulk differs, and AI used for scripts or visuals to deliver "a unique, well-researched, or creative narrative". The fetched text does not mention AI voices at all. YPP page: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public long-form watch hours (12 months) or 10 million Shorts views (90 days); "review typically takes about 1 month"; an update starts 1 Feb 2027 and its thresholds are not stated.
- **Secondary only (UNVERIFIED):** blogs describe an early tier at 500 subscribers and say the bar rises to 8,000 hours or 20 million views on 1 Feb 2027. The official page shows neither. Do not plan around them until Google confirms.
- **Secondary (medium confidence):** July 2026 clarifications split non-monetizable content into generic or template-based, distressing or manipulative, and AI personas on sensitive topics; enforcement is described as channel-level. Sources: TechCrunch, Logie, HackerNoon, AIR.
- **Test & Compare (secondary, 2025):** thumbnails can be tested on public long videos, up to three variants, results in days to two weeks; title testing was rolling out to a small share of creators. Not drivable through the API; it is a Studio feature.
- **Shorts vs long form (low-confidence marketing blogs):** Shorts discover, long videos convert (about 0.1 to 0.5% against 1 to 3% subscriber conversion); Shorts-gained subscribers often skip long videos. Our own numbers agree with the direction: nearly all views and subscribers come from Shorts.

## 6. What to do next
**Now (days, mostly small):**
1. **Slow the publishing rate** to about 3 to 5 videos a week until the channel has an audience. This is the cheapest fix for O1 and O3 and your decision to make. Settings `uploads_per_day` and `shorts_per_day`.
2. **Source-strength gate for Shorts:** refuse a Short whose source article is short or whose script says its sources say little (O4). Small code change.
3. **Cap AI pictures** per video, prefer archive images, reuse pictures across Shorts (O3).
4. **Commit a `docs/SERVER_STATE.md`** listing every server-only setting (no secrets) and the playlist ids (O8).
5. **Rotate the history question:** decide whether to scan or purge the unvetted skill from the public history (A11).

**Next (weeks):**
6. **Make "original insight" structural** (the official test): add a short "what is known, what is legend, what is disputed" segment to every documentary and show sources on screen. This is the policy's own wording for what makes AI-assisted work acceptable.
7. **Turn `script_retells` into a repair** (the fixer rewrites the later chapter).
8. **Use Test & Compare in Studio by hand** on the two best documentaries, then feed the winner back into the title and thumbnail rules.
9. **Per-video retention in the re-check:** flag a documentary people leave in the first 30 seconds.
10. **Backups:** nightly copy of `data/`, `token.json` (encrypted) and the schedule to another place.

**Later:** more claim-level verification against primary sources; a second sister channel only after one channel shows an audience; community posts and comment replies (the permission exists but replies written by a model need a human rule first).
