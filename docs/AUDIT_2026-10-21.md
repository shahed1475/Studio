# Platform audit, 21 Oct 2026 (Growth, Autopilot, pipeline, dashboard, risks)

Method: read the code, the server state (`output/`, `data/agent/`, logs), the Risks monitor, and every dashboard page in the browser (Videos, a video's detail page, Autopilot, Growth, Create, YouTube, Settings, Home). Pages and buttons that act on the channel or spend money were not clicked. Labels: VERIFIED = seen in code or server state today.

## 1. Does Autopilot follow Growth before it works?
VERIFIED: **no, not directly.** `autopilot.py` and `planner.py` never read Growth's analysis or its strategies. The two meet only at the topic queue:
- Growth (`agent.py`, daily) fills the queue (`queue_ideas`, applied `topic` strategies) and studies reference channels and trends.
- Autopilot takes the top topic with `topicrank.pop_best`, which scores the queue by word overlap with well-viewed titles from the trend and channel studies.
- Strategies of kind title, format, schedule, thumbnail are advice for the owner. Nothing applies them. 39 of them are listed, many duplicates.
- There is no feedback: Growth never sees which videos failed review, why, or what they cost. Own channel numbers are 0 subscribers, 0 views, so Growth has nothing of its own to learn from yet.
