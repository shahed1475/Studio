## Niche (owner's choice: all three, under one promise)
Promise: **"Free or cheap tools that save you time, explained plainly."** One channel, three series (playlists), started in this order:
1. **Free and open-source alternatives** (first: facts come from the GitHub API with dates, so every claim is checkable; search-friendly titles such as "free alternative to X").

3. **No-code and automation** (needs screen recordings: later, after a recording step exists).
Risk of mixing three: a scattered channel. Rule: series 1 only for the first 10 videos; series 2 and 3 start after that.

## Why this niche (and what is NOT proven)
- Search intent is clear: YouTube autocomplete (read 25 Oct) shows people type "open source alternatives to adobe / notion / power bi", "self hosted ai / vpn / security camera system", "ai tools for business / content creation / students", "automation with n8n / make.com". Autocomplete shows what is typed, not how often.
- Guides rank AI tools, software tutorials and no-code as strong 2026 niches, but their growth and CPM numbers come from vendor blogs. The measured data we found (AIR Media-Tech, about 300 channels, May 2025 to May 2026) puts Gadgets & Tech at a median RPM of $2.33 and Studio CPM of $5.73, with no developer-tools split: do not plan on a developer premium.
- Not found: sourced audience data for open-source and self-hosted topics.

## Video formats
- Short (under 60 s): "Free alternative to {paid tool}: {tool}", one fact, one limitation, link in the description.
- Explainer (6 to 10 min): "{Paid tool} vs {free tool}: what you really lose", with a dated facts card (stars, last release, licence) from the GitHub API.
- Titles: honest, specific, the tool name early; no "you won't believe"; thumbnails: tool logo area left empty of trademarks unless the licence allows, 3 to 4 words.
