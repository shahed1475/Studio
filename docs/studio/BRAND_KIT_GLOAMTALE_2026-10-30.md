# Gloamtale: brand kit (30 Oct 2026)

Made at the owner's request ("create a brand name and logo and set everything, eye catching, attractive, professional, a small name people remember"). Nothing here has been applied to the YouTube channel: that needs the owner's yes and is done by hand in YouTube Studio.

## The name
**Gloamtale**, handle **@Gloamtale**. "Gloam" is the old word for twilight, "tale" is the story: stories at dusk, for the end of the day. Two syllables to say, nine letters, spelled as heard.
Checked on YouTube's official search on 30 Oct 2026 (name: no channel titled exactly "Gloamtale" among the top 10 results; handle: the API finds none): free. Sixteen other candidates all had a clash, including Dusklore, Hushlore, Lorelight, Emberlore, Duskhaven, Duskwick, Gloaming. Tarnlight was also free but says nothing about stories.
**Not checked:** trademarks, domains, other platforms. Search a trademark database and register the handle on other platforms before investing in merchandise or a website. YouTube's limits on how often a name or handle can change should be read in Studio before changing.
YouTube name field (skill rule: brand plus a searchable topic): **Gloamtale: History & Legends**.

## The mark
A gold **G that is also a setting sun**: the ring is the sun, the bar of the G is the horizon, and two dunes sit inside. One shape, readable at 48 px, works on dark and light video. Files in `branding/gloamtale/`: `mark.svg` (vector), `mark-1000.png`, `avatar-800.png`, `banner-2560x1440.png`, `watermark-150.png`, `lockup.png`; `make_brand.py` redraws all of them; `brand.json` holds the tokens.

## Palette (from one dusk sky)
night `#0b0f2a`, indigo `#1b2150`, plum `#3b2350`, far dune `#4a2a4a`, mid dune `#2a1a3a`, near dune `#140e2a`, ember `#d7743a`, **gold `#f2c46d`** (the mark and accents), **cream `#fff1d4`** (the wordmark and text). One accent, gold. Ember only on the horizon glow and inside the mark.

## Type
Cinzel (inscribed Roman capitals, SIL Open Font License, licence file kept in `fonts/`), weight 700 for the wordmark with wide tracking, weight 500 for the tagline. Body text on any web page: the system sans.

## Tagline and text
- Banner tagline: **History & legends told at dusk** (short enough to read on a phone; ends 102 px inside the safe area).
- About (the first two lines carry the value, as the channel-optimizer skill requires):
  > History and legends for the end of the day. Slow, calm stories about lost armies, forgotten kings and old mysteries, told in plain English from real sources.
  >
  > Each story separates what is documented from what is legend, names its sources in the description and marks its pictures as AI-made. New stories every week, made to watch with the lights low or to fall asleep to. Start with the newest video, or search for a legend you have always wondered about.
- The weekly cadence matches the pace governor (three documentaries and seven Shorts a week at most); say it only while that stays true.
