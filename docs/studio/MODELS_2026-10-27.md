## Not allowed yet
- Seedance 2.x: OpenRouter prices it per video token, not per second. The tool refuses it ("priced per video token") until one clip has been measured and a per-second figure is known.
- Anything not in `video_generation_allowed`.

## Limits of this evidence
- The thumbnail model test used two scenes; the choice is a judgement, not a proof.
- Competitor and thumbnail craft rules come from third-party sources (not official YouTube guidance) and from the competitor sample in `GROWTH_PLAN_2026-10-27.md`.
- A vision score is a model's opinion; the real test is YouTube's click rate in Studio.

## Thumbnail rules built into the prompts and the scorer
One clear subject, a strong mood, rich contrast, 1 to 5 big bold words that add something the title does not say, readable at phone size (168 px), nothing important in the
bottom-right corner (the duration badge), 1280x720 under 2 MB, the picture and words must match the story, AI pictures are credited. The scorer checks each with a 0 to 10 rubric
(`thumbscore.PARTS`); a thumbnail that does not fit the story is capped at 3.

## Animation and 3D (27 Oct 2026)
`python run.py remake <slug> --style 3d --animate N` builds a NEW held video of the same story from a saved documentary script: the drawn scenes get a 3D animated-film look, documented
real pictures (museum items, maps, prints) stay real and credited, and N drawn scenes (spread across the video) become 8-second clips through image to video. The old video is untouched;
the new one is held and not approved. Rules: only scenes the studio drew are animated; a clip made before is reused from the cache; a failed clip leaves the picture; two failures in a row stop
the buying; the video's budget defaults to $3 (`animate_budget`); the clips are credited as AI-generated video. Code: `animate.py`, `run.cmd_remake`, `aiart.generate(style="3d")`.
